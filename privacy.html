Privacy Policy — Quadle

Last updated: 8 September 2026 · Cooperkite Games

Quadle is a puzzle game. It is built to need as little of your information as possible. This page explains exactly what it does and does not do.

What we do not collect

  • No account, no sign-up, no email address, no password.
  • No name, phone number, address or date of birth.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • No location of any kind, precise or approximate.

What stays on your device

Your streak, which puzzles you have finished, your solve times and your settings are stored on your phone. They are not sent to us. If you delete the app, they are gone.

Backing up your progress

If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games (Android) or use iCloud (iOS), your progress is saved to your own account with that provider so you can move to a new phone. That copy is held by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies, not by us. Signing in is optional and the game works fully without it.

Optional puzzle statistics

In Settings you can turn on Help improve the puzzles. It is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, the app records, for each board you play: which game, the grid size, the difficulty level, how long you spent, how many hints you used, and whether you finished. Nothing else. It is not linked to you, because there is nothing to link it to. You can turn it off at any time and the record stops.

Advertising

Quadle shows ads to support the free version. Ad providers may use a device advertising identifier to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure whether an ad worked. You can reset or delete that identifier in your phone's settings at any time, and you will be asked for consent where the law requires it. If you buy the ad-free version, no ads are requested and no ad identifier is used.

Children

Quadle is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn puzzle statistics off in Settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Remove any cloud backup through your Google or Apple account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
